\chapter{Acknowledgement of generative AI assistance}
I used GPT-5.6-Sol and GPT-6-Astra through tools including OpenAI Codex and Cursor. They helped with preparing and revising explanatory text from project notes, supervision feedback and recorded results, as well as language editing, translation, summaries, literature searches, discussion of research and comparison methods, and \LaTeX{} equation formatting.

AI also assisted with writing, revising and debugging parts of the research code, running tests, and preparing plotting and explanatory-diagram code and layout. This involved repeated instructions and revisions against the project requirements. The reported findings come from job data and saved program outputs; technical claims refer to original papers and official documentation, not AI responses.

I acknowledge this assistance rather than presenting the relevant work as wholly unaided. Review included code tests, source checks and comparisons with saved results. The dissertation reports unresolved limitations. I take responsibility for the submitted work, its attribution and interpretation. Conversation histories, revision records and test logs document the assistance provided.
